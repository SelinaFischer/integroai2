About the AI Readiness Assessment

The AI Readiness Assessment from IntegroAI is a free self-check for business owners and leaders. Your results show your readiness stage, your strongest and weakest areas, and one practical first step with a ready-to-use AI prompt.

Questions business owners ask

What is an AI readiness assessment?

A short check of whether your business is ready to get value from AI, before you choose tools or commit budget. It shows where the gaps are and what to fix first.

How long does it take and what does it cost?

It is free and takes about 5 minutes: 10 multiple-choice questions.

What if my score is low?

A low score is useful. It shows what to fix before you spend money on AI, which is far cheaper than fixing it afterwards.
